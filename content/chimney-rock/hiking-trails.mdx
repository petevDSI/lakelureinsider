Chimney Rock State Park has more trail mileage than most visitors realize, split across three separate access points. Most people only ever see the seven trails inside the main, paid-admission area near Chimney Rock itself — but there are three more, free to hike, on the far side of the gorge.

Distances and difficulty ratings below come directly from the park's own official trail data (NC State Parks) — not estimates.

## Main Park Trails (Paid Access)

These start from inside the ticketed area, near the elevator and Chimney Rock itself.

**Riverwalk Trail** — <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.riverwalk.distance" />, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.riverwalk.difficulty" />. A short, flat path along the river — good for a quick stretch or with very young kids.

**Great Woodland Adventure Trail** (also called the K.I.P. Track) — <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.great-woodland-adventure.distance" />, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.great-woodland-adventure.difficulty" />. A loop built for families, close to the base area.

**Four Seasons Trail** — <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.four-seasons.distance" />, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.four-seasons.difficulty" />. Short but steep enough to earn its rating.

**Hickory Nut Falls Trail** — <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.hickory-nut-falls.distance" />, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.hickory-nut-falls.difficulty" />, about <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.hickory-nut-falls.time" />. Leads to the base of the falls. Full details on our [Hickory Nut Falls page](/chimney-rock/hickory-nut-falls).

**Outcroppings Trail** — <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.outcroppings.distance" />, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.outcroppings.difficulty" />. Short and steep, winding among the rock formations below the summit.

**Exclamation Point Trail** — <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.exclamation-point.distance" />, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.exclamation-point.difficulty" />. Ends at one of the park's best overlooks and is also the starting point for Skyline Trail.

**Skyline Trail** — <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.skyline.distance" />, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.skyline.difficulty" />, about <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.skyline.time" />. The park's backcountry ridgeline trail, ending at Peregrine's Point (2,640 ft) with the top-down view of Hickory Nut Falls along the way.

<InsiderTip>
Trail closing times aren't the same as the park's closing time. Skyline Trail closes at <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.skyline.close" /> and Hickory Nut Falls Trail closes at <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.hickory-nut-falls.close" /> during main season — both well before the park itself closes. If either trail is the reason for your visit, build your day around the trail cutoff, not the park's.
</InsiderTip>

## Free-Access Trails

Two more access points reach trails outside the main ticketed area — no admission required for these.

**Rumbling Bald Trail** — <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.rumbling-bald.distance" />, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.rumbling-bald.difficulty" />. Reached from the Rumbling Bald community side of the gorge.

**Weed Patch Mountain Trail** and **Tunnel Trail** — <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.weed-patch-mountain.distance" /> (<Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.weed-patch-mountain.difficulty" />) and <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.tunnel.distance" /> (<Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.tunnel.difficulty" />) respectively. Both are reached through Buffalo Creek Park's Eagle Rock Access, at 2191 State Road 1314, Lake Lure — managed by the Town of Lake Lure rather than the state park.

**Current status:** <Fact id="chimney-rock.trails.eagle-rock-access.status" />. Since Weed Patch Mountain and Tunnel trails are only reached through this entrance, both are effectively unreachable while it's closed. This is the kind of thing that changes without much warning — call ahead rather than planning a trip around it.
