Hickory Nut Falls drops <Fact id="chimney-rock.hickory-nut-falls.height.feet" /> feet down the gorge wall inside Chimney Rock State Park — <Fact id="chimney-rock.hickory-nut-falls.ranking" />. It's one of the park's signature sights, and one of the few places where a short, moderate hike gets you somewhere genuinely dramatic.

## Two Ways to See It

**From the base** — Hickory Nut Falls Trail, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.hickory-nut-falls.distance" />, rated <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.hickory-nut-falls.difficulty" />, about <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.hickory-nut-falls.time" /> round trip. This is the shorter, easier route, and the one most visitors take. The park describes the terrain as gently rolling with a few moderate uphill sections, some rocky ground, and a small set of stairs right at the base. It's recommended for families with small children — though not for strollers or wheelchairs. The base itself sits in what the park calls a rare, unique micro-ecosystem, with plant and amphibian species you won't see elsewhere in the park.

**From the top** — Skyline Trail, <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.skyline.distance" />, rated <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.skyline.difficulty" />, about <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.skyline.time" />. This is the park's longer backcountry ridgeline trail, and it passes the top of the falls on its way to Peregrine's Point (2,640 feet — the park's highest overlook). It's a meaningfully longer, harder hike than the base trail — go this way for the views along the ridge, not as a shortcut to the waterfall.

<InsiderTip>
If you only have time for one, the base trail is the better choice for most people — shorter, easier, and you're standing right at the waterfall rather than looking down at the top of it. Save Skyline Trail for a visit when you have 2+ hours and actually want the ridgeline hike, not just the falls.
</InsiderTip>

## Trail Hours

The Hickory Nut Falls Trail closes at <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.hickory-nut-falls.close" /> during main season (March 8–November 1) — earlier than the park's overall closing time. Give yourself enough buffer before that cutoff; the trail gate closes to new entries at that time regardless of when the park itself closes. See our [Tickets & Hours guide](/chimney-rock/tickets-and-hours) for the full seasonal schedule.

## On Screen

The falls and the cliffs around them were used as filming locations for *The Last of the Mohicans* (1992) — one of the more recognizable pieces of Hollywood history in the North Carolina mountains.
