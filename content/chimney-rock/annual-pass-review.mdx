Adult day ticket: <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.adult-day" />. Adult annual pass: <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.adult-annual" />. The difference is $15 — which is exactly the same-day upgrade cost. Stop at the ticket window on your way out and your next visit costs nothing.

That's the core of it. Everything below is the fine print and family math.

## The Upgrade Path

You don't have to decide before you walk in. Buy a standard day ticket, do the park, and if you're at the top thinking you'll come back — stop at the ticket window on the way out and pay the $15 upgrade.

You have up to 30 days with your original receipt if you need to decide later, but the same-day upgrade is the cleanest option.

Upgrade rates: **<Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.adult-upgrade" /> per adult** (from <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.adult-day" /> day → <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.adult-annual" /> annual), **<Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.youth-upgrade" /> per youth** (from <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.youth-day" /> day → <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.youth-annual" /> annual). Children 4 and under are always free.

<InsiderTip>
The 30-day window is underused. If you're staying in the gorge for a few days and visit on day one, you can decide on the upgrade any time before you leave the area — you don't have to commit at the gate or on the way out. Keep the receipt.
</InsiderTip>

## Individual Pass Break-Even

One day ticket: <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.adult-day" />. Annual pass: <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.adult-annual" />. Break-even: the second visit.

Two day visits total $34. The annual pass is $32. If you use the upgrade on your first visit ($17 + $15 = $32), the second visit costs $0. Either way you come out ahead on visit two.

The math is identical for youth: <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.youth-day" /> day, <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.youth-annual" /> annual, <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.youth-upgrade" /> upgrade. Two visits and the annual is ahead.

## Family Pass Math

The family annual pass is <Fact id="chimney-rock.admission.family-annual" /> and covers 2 adults + up to 3 youth (ages 5–15).

**The edge case worth knowing:** A family of 2 adults + 1 youth pays $78 in individual annual passes (2×$32 + 1×$14). The family pass is $80 — **$2 more than buying individually.** For exactly this family size, the family pass is the worse deal.

For 2 adults + 2 youth, individual annual passes total $92; the family pass is $80 — $12 savings. For 2 adults + 3 youth, individual totals $106; family pass $80 — $26 savings.

There is no published family upgrade rate. If you want the family pass, you buy it outright.

## What the Pass Includes Beyond Admission

- Unlimited visits for 12 months from purchase month
- $2 off each adult and $1 off each child ticket for guests you bring in
- 15% off at Cliff Dwellers Gifts and the Sky Lounge Gift Shop & Deli
- Passholder-only events and discounts on guided hikes and workshops
- Partner discounts at other area attractions (published annually as a PDF on the park website)
- No reservations required, ever

The guest discount adds up if you're local and bring different people throughout the year.
