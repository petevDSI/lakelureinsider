Lake Lure is a 720-acre reservoir in Rutherford County, NC, surrounded by the Blue Ridge foothills and anchored by the Hickory Nut Gorge. The town sits at the eastern end of the lake; Chimney Rock State Park is 3.2 miles west.

The lake became famous when the 1987 film _Dirty Dancing_ was shot here — the lift scene happened on this water. The Lake Lure Inn still has its Roosevelt Hall ballroom from that era.
