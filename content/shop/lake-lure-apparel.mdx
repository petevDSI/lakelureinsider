## The Stories Behind the Designs

### Edmund the goat

Edmund is a horned, shaggy-coated Kiko goat who showed up this summer in a goat fence along Highway 64/74A near the Rocky Broad River and has been crossing the river ever since. A viral campaign poster made him a write-in "candidate," and the designs borrow from that: the red bow tie and rosette on the Edmund for Mayor tees, "Lake Life. Goat Life." on the heavyweight tee, the "Don't Fence Me In" line from a viral Edmund reel, and the "Escape Artist" hoodie, whose back print is a nod to all the #FreeEdmund updates. Read the whole story in <a href="/news/edmund-goat-mayor" className="font-semibold text-(--lake) underline">Meet Edmund</a>.

### 100 Dam Years

The Lake Lure Dam turns 100 in 2026, and the whole lake exists because of it. The goat float designs put Edmund on a yellow inner tube in front of the dam, doing exactly what everyone else does at Lake Lure in July. The Dam Blueprint Tee is the other side of that coin: engineering-style linework of the dam under a "Structure No. 1926" title block, printed light on dark colors. The centennial badge, a sunrise over the Blue Ridge, is on the crewneck sweatshirt and the heavyweight long sleeve.

### Chimney Rock and the lake

Several designs put Edmund against the places visitors actually come for: floating chest-deep in the lake with Chimney Rock behind him, or silhouetted atop the rock at sunset on the hoodie. Wear it where you took the photo.
