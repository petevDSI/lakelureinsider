## How Long Your Order Takes

Your order has two stages: printing, then shipping.

- **Printing:** your item is made after you order. Printful's average fulfillment time is <Fact id="shop.production-time" />.
- **Shipping, standard:** about <Fact id="shop.transit-standard" /> in the USA once the order has shipped.
- **Shipping, express:** about <Fact id="shop.transit-express" /> in the USA, when offered at checkout.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Timing varies with the item, how busy production is, and where you live. Your exact delivery estimate is shown at checkout before you pay, and you will get tracking by email once the order ships.

## What Shipping Costs

Shipping is calculated at checkout from your address and the shipping method you choose. There is no flat fee to guess at: you see the real amount before you pay.

## Returns, Reprints, and Refunds

Because every item is printed for you, we follow the same terms as our print partner:

**Covered**
- **Damaged, defective, or misprinted items:** tell us within <Fact id="shop.return-window" /> of delivery and we will arrange a reprint or a refund. Photos of the problem help.
- **Lost packages:** report it within <Fact id="shop.return-window" /> of the estimated delivery date.
- **Delivery problems:** if a package comes back because of a carrier error or an address problem, it can be reshipped.

**Not accepted**
- Returns or exchanges for size, color, or change of mind (buyer's remorse). Every item is made to order, so we cannot restock it.

The best protection against a wrong size is the size chart on each item page. Our <a href="/shop/lake-lure-apparel" className="font-semibold text-(--lake) underline">Lake Lure apparel page</a> links to every shirt, sweatshirt, hoodie, and hat, and each one lists its sizes and prices.
