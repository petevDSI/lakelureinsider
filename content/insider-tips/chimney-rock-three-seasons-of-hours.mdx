Search for Chimney Rock's hours and you'll usually find one clean schedule. The park actually runs on three, plus holiday exceptions, and the shifts catch people off guard.

From <Fact id="chimney-rock.season.fall-winter.range" />, everything shifts earlier. Entry closes at <Fact id="chimney-rock.hours.fallwinter.entry-close" /> and the park closes at <Fact id="chimney-rock.hours.fallwinter.park-close" />. The late-afternoon trick still exists, but the whole window slides earlier, and daylight is shorter anyway.

Note where the switch happens. Main season runs through November 1, and the fall and winter schedule begins the next day, so a visit on the first weekend of November is already on the shorter clock.

Check which season your visit lands in before you plan the day. The same Saturday plan that works in October, with a mid-afternoon arrival and a trail after it, can run out of daylight and entry time in November.
