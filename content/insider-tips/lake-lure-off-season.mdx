Most of what's written about Lake Lure assumes you're coming in summer. If you're visiting from October through spring, a few things are closed and a few things matter more than they do in July.

## What's Seasonal

**The beach.** Lake Lure Beach's 2026 season ended on <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.season.end" />. Expect it back <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.reopens" />, based on previous years, but the Town hasn't announced 2027 dates, so treat that as an expectation rather than a promise. Our [beach guide](/insider-tips/lake-lure-beach-season-and-fees) has the details.

**Boat rentals.** One operator describes its schedule this way: <Fact id="boat-rental.lake-lure-adventure-company.season" />. Others may differ, so call before you build a day around the water. See the [boat rental directory](/things-to-do/boat-rentals) for the operators we track.

## What Doesn't Depend on the Season

- **The Flowering Bridge gardens** are <Fact id="flowering-bridge.gardens.status" />. See our [gardens tip](/insider-tips/flowering-bridge-gardens-still-open).
- **Chimney Rock State Park** keeps running, on a different clock. The fall and winter schedule covers <Fact id="chimney-rock.season.fall-winter.range" />, and early-year hours are not published at all. Read our [guide to the three sets of hours](/insider-tips/chimney-rock-three-seasons-of-hours) before you plan around it.
- **Rock climbing.** The free Rumbling Bald climbing area has its popular season in fall and winter, since the south-facing rock gets hot in summer. See the [first-timer tip](/insider-tips/first-time-climbing-rumbling-bald).
- **Apple orchards** are a fall draw. See our tip on the [orchards along Chimney Rock Road](/insider-tips/apple-orchards-on-chimney-rock-road).
