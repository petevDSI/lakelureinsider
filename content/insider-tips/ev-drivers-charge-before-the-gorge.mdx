If you're arriving in an electric vehicle, the charging situation in the gorge is thin, and the time to solve it is before you leave home.

## What's Actually Here

**Lake Lure.** There's one station, at Morse Park next to the Welcome Center and Town Hall: two Level 2 ports, open around the clock. It's paid, and it works through the ChargeUP Driver app. It was installed in June 2026 and replaced the Town's earlier free station at the same spot.

Two ports is not much, and the Town has previously posted notices about downtime, so don't make the plan depend on it being available.

**Chimney Rock State Park.** As of <Fact id="chimney-rock.ev-charging.none-found" />, we found no public charger on site. The Town's page lists only the Lake Lure station.

## Where the Faster Options Are

Anything quicker than Level 2 means driving out of the gorge:

- **Hendersonville** has a free ChargePoint lot downtown with Level 2 ports. It's <Fact id="drive.lake-lure-to-hendersonville.minutes" /> from Lake Lure by road. Both ports were showing available at our last check.
- **Asheville** has a Tesla Supercharger site and a dealership lot with DC fast charging. It's <Fact id="drive.asheville-to-lake-lure.minutes" /> from Lake Lure.
- **Black Mountain** has a small free station at the police department, but it needs an app and doesn't report live status.

Our [EV charging guide](/trip-planning/ev-charging) lists each station with its network, port count, and connector type.

## A Simple Plan

1. **Arrive with enough range to cover the trip in, the day's driving, and the trip out.** Treat the Lake Lure charger as a bonus.
2. **Charge at your base town or on the way in.** A Level 2 stop that fits a meal or a shopping stop is the easiest way to top up.
3. **Check port availability the day you travel.** Station status changes, and these listings were last verified in late summer 2026.

<InsiderTip>
Cold weather and steep mountain driving both reduce an EV's range. If your trip is in late fall or winter, or you're heading up toward the gorge, leave yourself a bigger margin than the trip computer suggests.
</InsiderTip>
