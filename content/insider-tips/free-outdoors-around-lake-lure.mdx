Chimney Rock State Park is the headline attraction, and it charges for entry. If you want to see the area without paying a ticket, or to fill the rest of a trip, you have more options than most visitors realize.

## Free to Visit

**The Flowering Bridge gardens.** <Fact id="flowering-bridge.gardens.status" />. The bridge itself is gone, but the gardens aren't. See our [full tip](/insider-tips/flowering-bridge-gardens-still-open).

**Short walks in town.** The Rutherford Outdoor Coalition lists a Lake Lure Town Center walkway, trails at Morse Park, and a riverwalk along the Rocky Broad, all short and rated easy. Our [trails directory](/things-to-do/trails) lists them with distances.

**The Chimney Rock Village boulders.** A short trail on Round Top Mountain leads to a set of boulder problems, big views, and a small seasonal waterfall. It's a Carolina Climbers Coalition access area on a trail easement through village land. Parking is at the public lot next to Burntshirt Vineyards or on Main Street.

**Rumbling Bald, the climbing area.** Free to climb, with a permit to fill out at the kiosk. See our [first-timer tip](/insider-tips/first-time-climbing-rumbling-bald), and don't confuse it with [the resort](/insider-tips/rumbling-bald-two-places).

## The One That's Closed

The state's current listing for Eagle Rock Access, the separate free-access area at Buffalo Creek Park, reads: <Fact id="chimney-rock.trails.eagle-rock-access.status" />. Older guides still list it as an option, so don't plan around it without checking. That closure doesn't affect the ticketed park.

<InsiderTip>
"Free" often still means a parking fee. Some of the village access points use a paid public lot, so bring a few dollars or plan to park on Main Street, and check the signage when you arrive.
</InsiderTip>

## A Note on Conditions

Hurricane Helene reshaped a lot of the terrain in this area. A trail listing that's a few years old may describe a route that's changed, so confirm current conditions with the managing organization before you rely on one.
