The beach at Lake Lure reopened for the 2026 season after a long post-Helene closure, and its schedule narrowed as summer went on. If you're planning a beach day, the calendar matters as much as the weather.

## How the 2026 Season Ran

The Town reported the lake as <Fact id="lake-lure.open.status" />. On the beach itself, the schedule was daily through <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.schedule.daily-through" />. The season ended on <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.season.end" />, and it is closed now.

The late-summer shift is the part visitors miss. A beach day in August can be a Tuesday; a beach day in early September is a weekend.

## Planning for 2027

Expect the beach back <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.reopens" />, based on previous years. The Town hasn't announced dates, so that's an expectation, not a schedule. Watch for the announcement in spring rather than planning a Memorial Day trip around it.

<InsiderTip>
The child rate has a height requirement as well as an age range, so measure before you drive out. A tall four-year-old and a short eleven-year-old can land on different sides of it.
</InsiderTip>
