Hurricane Helene hit this corner of North Carolina in late September 2024, and the area came back in pieces rather than all at once. Many pages still describe an earlier version of what's open. This is the short version of what we've verified, with the dates attached.

Everything below was confirmed from the source named; we have not re-checked it today, so confirm anything you're building a trip around.

## What Has Reopened

- **Chimney Rock State Park.** Reopened: <Fact id="chimney-rock.open.reopened" />. Elevator status at our last check: <Fact id="chimney-rock.elevator.operational" />.
- **Lake Lure.** <Fact id="lake-lure.open.status" />, after a <Fact id="lake-lure.open.closure-duration" />.
- **US 64.** <Fact id="road.us64.status" />.
- **Washburn Marina.** <Fact id="washburn-marina.open.status" />. Fuel service: <Fact id="washburn-marina.fuel.status" /> (as of early August 2026), so call before counting on it.

## What's Still Closed or Unresolved

- **Eagle Rock Access (Buffalo Creek Park).** The state lists it as <Fact id="chimney-rock.trails.eagle-rock-access.status" />. It's a separate, free-access area, so its closure doesn't affect the ticketed park.
- **The Flowering Bridge.** The bridge itself was <Fact id="flowering-bridge.bridge.status" />. The surrounding gardens are a different story, and we cover them in our [Flowering Bridge gardens tip](/insider-tips/flowering-bridge-gardens-still-open).
- **Guided climbing on the Chimney Rock monolith.** It was on hold when we last checked; the free Rumbling Bald climbing area is the open alternative.

## What's Still Under Construction

Rebuilding is ongoing. Our reporting lists <Fact id="chimney-rock.road.reconstruction-2029" />, and the Town has a replacement-dam project in design, described as <Fact id="construction.replacement-dam.status" />.

<InsiderTip>
Dates on tourism pages drift quickly during a recovery. When a listing says something is closed "until further notice," check the operator's own site or call, because nothing tells you when a status line was last updated. We note the verification date on our own pages for exactly that reason.
</InsiderTip>
