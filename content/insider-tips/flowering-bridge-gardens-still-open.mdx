If you search for the Flowering Bridge, you'll find a lot of pages that describe it as a walkable garden bridge over the Rocky Broad River in the middle of Lake Lure. That description is out of date.

## What Happened

The organization that runs the site states plainly that the bridge is gone: <Fact id="flowering-bridge.bridge.status" />. A replacement is not a settled matter either. According to the same source, the status is <Fact id="flowering-bridge.replacement.status" />.

## What's Still There

The gardens are separate from the bridge, and they have stayed open. Per the organization's FAQ, they are <Fact id="flowering-bridge.gardens.status" />.

The address it gives is <Fact id="flowering-bridge.gardens.address" />.

So you can still walk the plantings on either bank, just without the bridge connecting them. Photos and descriptions online often predate the storm, so expect the place to look different from what you've seen.

## Making the Stop Work

- **It costs nothing, and it isn't tied to opening hours.** That makes it an easy fit for the gaps in a day, such as the stretch before a dinner reservation or a morning when other things haven't opened yet.
- **It's a good off-season stop.** The Town's beach is closed outside summer, but the gardens aren't on a seasonal schedule. Our [off-season guide](/insider-tips/lake-lure-off-season) lists what else stays available.
- **Check the garden organization's site before you go.** The status of a recovering site can change, and the people who maintain it will know first.
