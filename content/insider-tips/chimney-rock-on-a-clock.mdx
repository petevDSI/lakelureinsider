Chimney Rock's entry deadline gets all the attention. What catches people is the next layer down: each trail has its own closing time, and they aren't the same.

## The Numbers That Matter

In main season, the park lists these cutoffs:

- **Skyline Trail** closes at <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.skyline.close" />
- **Hickory Nut Falls Trail** closes at <Fact id="chimney-rock.trail.hickory-nut-falls.close" />
- **Entry** closes at <Fact id="chimney-rock.hours.main.entry-close" />, and the **park** at <Fact id="chimney-rock.hours.main.park-close" />

So the trail with the longer hike also closes first, and it closes before the entry deadline has even passed. Someone who walks in at the last moment can no longer start Skyline at all.

## Sequencing a Short Visit

1. **Decide whether you want Skyline at all.** If you do, it gets the early slot, because its closing time is the one you can't beat by hurrying.
2. **Take the elevator when daylight is the constraint.** It's <Fact id="chimney-rock.elevator.operational" />, and it gets you to the summit without spending trail time.
3. **Save Hickory Nut Falls for last.** It closes later and takes less time, so it's the one that fits into the end of the day.

Do the sums with your own pace and your own group, and leave margin. Kids and photo stops stretch a trail faster than a trail page assumes.

<InsiderTip>
These cutoffs are the main-season ones. The park publishes a different, earlier set of hours from November 2 on, and we haven't found trail-by-trail closing times for that stretch. If your trip is in the fall or winter schedule, confirm the trail times with the park. Our guide to the [three sets of hours](/insider-tips/chimney-rock-three-seasons-of-hours) explains the calendar.
