Chimney Rock is more dog-friendly than many ticketed attractions, with one rule that reshapes the whole visit.

## The Two Rules

On the trails: <Fact id="chimney-rock.dogs.trails" />.

In the elevator: <Fact id="chimney-rock.dogs.elevator" />.

The source for both is the park's website and the NC State Parks site, checked in early August 2026.

## What That Means for Your Day

The elevator is the easiest way to the summit, and it's off the table with a dog. That doesn't close off the views, since the trails are open to leashed dogs, but it does mean you should choose your route deliberately rather than deciding at the elevator doors.

A few practical consequences:

- **Pick the trail first, then the schedule.** The [Hickory Nut Falls Trail](/chimney-rock/hickory-nut-falls) and the longer Skyline hike are both trails, so both work with a dog on a leash. Our [clock guide](/insider-tips/chimney-rock-on-a-clock) covers when each one closes.
- **Split the group only if you have to.** If some of your party wants the elevator, someone has to stay with the dog, since neither can go through the elevator or into the Sky Lounge. Settle that before you reach the ticket plaza.
- **Keep the leash short.** The limit is a leash no longer than six feet, and on narrow trails with other hikers, a shorter hold is easier anyway.

<InsiderTip>
Check the weather and the surface before bringing a dog on a long trail. We don't publish conditions underfoot, and a hot afternoon on a rocky, steep path can be harder on a dog than the distance suggests.
</InsiderTip>
