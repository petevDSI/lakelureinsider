Parts of the 1987 film *Dirty Dancing* were shot around Lake Lure, and a narrated boat tour built around the film runs on the lake. The most useful thing to know about it is a logistics detail that catches people out.

## Where It Actually Departs

If you've been looking at Washburn Marina, stop. Local sources confirmed to us that the tour leaves from <Fact id="lake-lure.tours.departure" />, as of August 2026.

That's an easy mistake to make. The tour is run by Lake Lure Tours, whose pontoon rental side is marina-based, so people assume the tour leaves from there too.

## Check the Schedule Before You Drive Out

The beach itself runs seasonally, as we explain in our [beach guide](/insider-tips/lake-lure-beach-season-and-fees). We haven't confirmed whether the tour keeps its summer schedule once the beach closes, so if you're coming outside summer, check with Lake Lure Tours rather than assuming it runs.

## The Inn

*Dirty Dancing* is also tied to the Lake Lure Inn. According to the Inn's own materials, its Roosevelt Hall was used for filming, with eight chandeliers and a grand fireplace, and it's now bookable for events. We haven't independently verified the filming details beyond what the Inn states, so treat the specifics as the Inn's account.

The Inn's spa was listed as <Fact id="lake-lure-inn.spa.status" /> when we last checked in early August, so check before booking a treatment.

<InsiderTip>
When you book, ask the operator to put the departure location in writing on your confirmation. If you can't find it in the booking details, call before the day of the tour rather than driving to the lake to find out.
</InsiderTip>
