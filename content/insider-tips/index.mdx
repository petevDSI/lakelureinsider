Most travel advice about Lake Lure and Chimney Rock tells you what exists — the elevator, the falls, the beach. This page is about the things that take a few visits to figure out on your own.

<InsiderTip>
The single most useful thing to know before visiting Chimney Rock: the gate closes at 5:30 PM (main season) for new entries, but the park stays open until 7:00 PM. That's 90 minutes of buffer. Arriving at 5:15 PM still gets you a Hickory Nut Falls hike and the elevator to the summit. The afternoon light in the gorge is also dramatically better than midday. This is consistently one of the best windows to visit, and almost nobody uses it.
</InsiderTip>

<InsiderTip>
On peak fall Saturdays (late September through early November), the main parking lot fills before 10 AM. If you arrive after that, you'll park in the overflow area and take a short shuttle — not a big deal, but plan for it. The lot often starts clearing again after 2 PM as early-morning visitors leave.
</InsiderTip>

<InsiderTip>
The annual pass ($32/adult) pays for itself on your second visit — two day tickets cost $34. Better: you can buy a day ticket and upgrade to a pass within 30 days, with your receipt, at the Ticket Plaza. The upgrade costs $15 (exactly the difference between $17 and $32). So you can decide whether to pass after your first visit, and the math is the same either way.
</InsiderTip>
