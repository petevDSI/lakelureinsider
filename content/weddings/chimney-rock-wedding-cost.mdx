A Chimney Rock wedding has three potential cost components: the ceremony permit fee, park admission for all guests, and optional reception venue rental. They combine differently depending on your guest count, which makes the total harder to estimate than it looks.

The calculator below computes the exact total for your numbers.

## The Insight Worth Knowing First

Before you use the calculator: **inviting a 10th guest saves money compared to 9 guests.**

At 9 guests, park admission is charged at the standard rate ($17/adult). At 10, group rates kick in ($14/adult). The group discount is applied retroactively to the whole group — so all 10 people pay $14 instead of $17. That saves $3 per person × 10 people = $30 on admission, minus the $17 extra admission for the new guest, for a net savings of $13.

A 9-guest all-adult ceremony: $200 fee + 9×$17 = **$353**  
A 10-guest all-adult ceremony: $200 fee + 10×$14 = **$340**

There are two other sweet spots: staying at 5 guests avoids the fee-tier jump to $200 at 6 (saves $42), and staying at 15 guests avoids the jump to $250 at 16 (saves $64).

## How the Ceremony Fee Works

The ceremony permit fee is a flat charge based on total guest count. It covers the right to hold a private ceremony at your chosen site and includes the park coordinator's time for the permitting process.

| Guest count | Fee |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | $175 |
| 6–15 | $200 |
| 16–20 | $250 |
| 21–25 | $275 |
| 26–30 | $300 |
| 31–40 | $350 |
| 41–50 | $400 |
| 51–60 | $450 |
| 61–70 | $500 |
| 71–100 | $550 |
| 101–150 | $600 |

The fee tiers jump at 6, 16, 21, 26, 31, 41, 51, 61, 71, and 101. If you're just below one of those thresholds, the calculation changes significantly when you add a guest. The calculator above flags those boundaries automatically.

## Reception Rental Options

The ceremony fee covers the ceremony itself. If you want a space for a reception or gathering after, you can add a venue rental.

**Ceremony only** — no additional charge. Your guests are inside the park and can use any public areas after the ceremony.

**Outdoor Classroom (2 hours, $150)** — a secluded forest alcove with benches, away from visitor traffic. Good for small cocktail receptions.

**Outdoor Classroom + Pavilion, half day (4 hours, $300)** — adds the covered Pavilion, which is the largest venue in the park and the only one with vehicle access. Best for groups that need shade, seating, and catering setup.

**Outdoor Classroom + Pavilion, full day (8 hours, $550)** — same spaces, all day. Allows a morning ceremony at any park site followed by an all-afternoon reception at the Pavilion.

<InsiderTip>
The Pavilion is the only venue at Chimney Rock with both vehicle access and handicap access. If any of your guests have mobility limitations, the Pavilion as a reception space lets everyone participate fully — even if the ceremony site itself requires stairs. A ceremony at the Hickory Nut Falls or the summit followed by a Pavilion reception is a workable combination for mixed-mobility groups.
</InsiderTip>
