The Hickory Nut Gorge offers two distinct wedding experiences: the dramatic outdoor settings of Chimney Rock State Park, and the graceful historic atmosphere of the Lake Lure Inn. They're 3.2 miles apart, and some couples use both — ceremony at the rock or falls, reception at the inn.

## Chimney Rock State Park

Chimney Rock hosts private wedding ceremonies at six outdoor sites. Each is inside the state park, which means your guests experience one of the most dramatic natural settings in the Blue Ridge Mountains.

The venues range from intimate to large: five sites hold 10–15 guests, while the Outdoor Classroom holds up to 70 and the fully accessible Pavilion accommodates up to 150. All guests pay park admission on top of the ceremony permit fee.

The most important number to know before you plan: **a 10-guest wedding costs less than a 9-guest wedding.** Park admission drops from $17 to $14 per adult when the group reaches 10, which more than offsets the extra admission. Use the calculator on the cost page to see the exact math for your guest count.

## Lake Lure Inn & Spa

The Lake Lure Inn opened in 1927 and has hosted guests and events ever since. Roosevelt Hall — the inn's main ballroom — was used as the filming location for the _Dirty Dancing_ (1987) dance scenes. It has eight chandeliers, 11-foot ceilings, and a grand fireplace.

The inn offers multiple spaces for ceremonies and receptions, from the private Powers Room (20 guests) to the full Dining Room, Veranda Terrace, and Poolside combination (215 guests). Horse and carriage for the newlyweds and a 32-passenger historic trolley for guest transport are available to add.
