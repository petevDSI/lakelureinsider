<EditorialIndependence />

Chimney Rock State Park offers six distinct outdoor ceremony sites. They occupy different positions inside the park — from the summit rock to the base of a 404-foot waterfall — and they differ significantly in access difficulty, maximum guest count, and atmosphere.

Every site is seasonal. Most are unavailable on holidays and holiday weekends. All require a ceremony permit, and all guests pay park admission.

## Comparison

| Venue | Max Guests | Access | Accessible | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chimney Rock | 15 | Elevator + 44 stairs | No | Dramatic summit ceremony |
| Hickory Nut Falls | 15 | 1.5-mi round-trip hike | No | Waterfall backdrop |
| Lake Lure Lookout | 10 | Elevator + short walk | No | Lake views, intimate |
| Outdoor Classroom | 70 | Walking path | Partial | Mid-size, shaded |
| The Pavilion | 150 | Vehicle + handicap access | Yes | Largest, accessible |
| Vista Rock | 15 | ~150 stairs up Outcroppings Trail | No | Privacy, gorge views |

## The Venues

**Chimney Rock** is the summit of the rock formation itself — the main observation platform at the top. Ceremonies here hold up to 15 guests. You take the elevator to the summit level, then climb 44 stairs to reach the ceremony spot. Not permitted on holidays or holiday weekends. The view is the full gorge in every direction.

**Hickory Nut Falls** is at the base of a 404-foot waterfall — the tallest in the eastern United States. Getting there requires a 1.5-mile round-trip hike on a moderate trail. All guests hike in together, which creates a processional-like experience. Holds up to 15 guests. Not permitted on holidays or holiday weekends. The waterfall backdrop is unlike anything else in the park.

**Lake Lure Lookout** is a small overlook between the Sky Lounge and the Chimney summit level. You reach it via the elevator, cross the patio, and descend a few steps. It holds up to 10 guests and offers a direct view of Lake Lure from the gorge rim. Seasonal; no holidays or holiday weekends.

**Outdoor Classroom** is a secluded forest alcove with wooden benches, away from the main visitor path. It's the largest natural ceremony site at 70 guests and the only one reachable by walking path without stairs. Also rentable for receptions (2 hours for $150, or combined with the Pavilion). Seasonal; no holidays or holiday weekends.

**The Pavilion** is the largest venue at 150 guests and the only one with both vehicle access and handicap access. It's a covered structure, making it viable in variable weather. Rental includes the Outdoor Classroom. The holiday restriction status of the Pavilion is unconfirmed — contact the park coordinator to verify before committing a date.

**Vista Rock** is reached by climbing the Outcroppings Trail, approximately 150 stairs. It's more secluded than the Chimney Rock summit and offers similar gorge views with less foot traffic from other park visitors. Holds up to 15 guests. Seasonal; no holidays or holiday weekends.

<InsiderTip>
If you have guests with limited mobility, the Pavilion is the only venue where everyone can participate in the ceremony itself. A practical alternative is to hold the ceremony at an accessible site (Pavilion or Outdoor Classroom) and let guests who are able to hike make an optional visit to the falls or summit separately. The park is a day-use site — you can do both on the same ticket.
</InsiderTip>
