The Lake Lure Inn opened in 1927 on the shoreline of Lake Lure, NC. For nearly a century it has been the primary gathering place for the area — and since 1987, it's been best known as the location where _Dirty Dancing_ was filmed.

The inn is now managed by Ridgeline Hotels under the name "Lake Lure Inn & Spa." The spa is temporarily closed, but the hotel and event spaces remain open for weddings and events.

## Roosevelt Hall — The Dirty Dancing Ballroom

Roosevelt Hall is the inn's main ballroom and the filming location for the dance sequences in _Dirty Dancing_ (1987). The room has eight chandeliers, 11-foot ceilings, and a grand fireplace. It holds up to 75 guests and is available for both ceremonies and receptions.

For many couples, Roosevelt Hall is the draw — the combination of the historic room and the film connection creates an atmosphere that photographs exceptionally well and gives guests a story to tell.

## All Venues

The inn offers several spaces that can be used individually or in combination:

**Roosevelt Hall** — 75 guests. Indoor. Eight chandeliers, 11-ft ceilings, grand fireplace. Used for the _Dirty Dancing_ (1987) dance scenes.

**Dining Room, Veranda Terrace, and Poolside** — up to 215 guests. The most popular combination for large weddings. The Dining Room and Veranda open onto the poolside terrace, creating an indoor-outdoor flow with lake views.

**Powers Room** — 20 guests. Private dining room with French doors. Good for rehearsal dinners or intimate ceremonies.

**Grand Lobby** — capacity varies. The historic hotel entrance with period architecture.

**Lakeside Gazebo** — capacity varies. Surrounded by meadows on the lakeshore. Available for outdoor ceremonies with a water backdrop.

**The Lawn** — capacity varies. Open lawn area; tents available for weather coverage.

## Extras

**Horse and carriage** is available for the newlyweds — a 1920s-era carriage on the inn's grounds or around the lakefront.

**32-passenger historic trolley** provides guest transportation within the inn's property and to and from nearby locations.
