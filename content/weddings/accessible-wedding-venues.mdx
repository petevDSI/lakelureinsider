Accessibility at Chimney Rock State Park depends heavily on which venue you choose. The park has an elevator that removes the main stair barrier, but only one ceremony site is fully accessible from end to end.

## The Pavilion — Fully Accessible

The Pavilion is the only Chimney Rock wedding venue with vehicle access and ADA-compliant handicap access. It's a covered structure that holds up to 150 guests and includes the adjacent Outdoor Classroom.

To reach the Pavilion: drive to the lower lot, where handicap parking is available, and walk directly to the venue. No stairs, no elevator required, no hiking. For guests who use wheelchairs, walkers, or have significant mobility limitations, this is the only venue where participation in the ceremony itself is possible without assistance.

The Pavilion's holiday restriction status is unconfirmed — verify with the park coordinator before committing a date.

## The Elevator — Partial Accessibility

The park elevator is included with admission and is fully accessible. It travels from the lower visitor level to the upper summit level in seconds, eliminating the long staircase that was previously the only route.

What the elevator makes accessible:
- The Sky Lounge and upper patio level
- The Lake Lure Lookout (a few steps from the elevator exit — not fully accessible, but manageable for many)
- The Outdoor Classroom (accessible by walking path from the upper level)

What the elevator does **not** make accessible:
- The Chimney Rock summit itself — approximately 44 stairs remain between the elevator landing and the ceremony spot on the rock
- Vista Rock — reached via the Outcroppings Trail (~150 stairs)
- Hickory Nut Falls — 1.5-mile round-trip hike on a moderate trail

## Practical Planning for Mixed-Mobility Groups

If some of your guests can navigate stairs or hike and others cannot, the Pavilion-as-ceremony-site is the most practical solution. Guests who want to experience the summit or falls can do so before or after the ceremony as park visitors — everyone attends the ceremony, and anyone who wants the full park experience can get it on their own timeline.

A full-day Pavilion rental ($550) gives you the Outdoor Classroom and Pavilion from morning through evening, with enough time for a ceremony plus reception and optional park exploration.

<InsiderTip>
If you're planning a small ceremony (under 15 guests) and most guests can handle stairs, the Lake Lure Lookout is worth considering for accessibility-adjacent groups. The elevator does the heavy lifting — only a short walk and a few stairs separate the elevator exit from the ceremony spot, and the lake views are exceptional.
</InsiderTip>
