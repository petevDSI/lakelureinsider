<QuickAnswer />

Lake Lure has been a golf town since 1927, when the Donald Ross-designed municipal course first opened — it's still there, still public, and still one of the most affordable rounds in the area. Rumbling Bald Resort adds two more, fuller 18-hole courses right in town. For a longer, more championship-scale round, Cleghorn Plantation in Rutherfordton is worth the extra drive.
