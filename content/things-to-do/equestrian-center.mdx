<QuickAnswer />

Tryon International Equestrian Center is a bigger destination than its name suggests. Yes, it's a serious competition venue — hunter/jumper, dressage, and eventing at an Olympic level, on the grounds that hosted the 2018 FEI World Equestrian Games — but it's also become a small resort in its own right, with eight restaurants, on-site lodging, golf and shooting sports, and a packed calendar of concerts and festivals alongside the riding. Most of the horse shows themselves are free to watch.
