Two trail systems overlap around here, and it's worth knowing which is which. **Chimney Rock State Park** has its own ten trails — some inside the paid-admission area, some free — with real-time closure status and turn-by-turn detail on our [Chimney Rock hiking trails guide](/chimney-rock/hiking-trails). **Everything else in the county** — greenways, nature preserves, game lands, waterfall hikes, and small-town walking paths — is covered by the countywide directory below, sourced from the Rutherford Outdoor Coalition.

## Planning a Hike

Trail conditions, closures, and access points change — especially after weather events. Call ahead or check current conditions before you go, particularly for anything reached through a free-access point rather than a maintained state park entrance.
