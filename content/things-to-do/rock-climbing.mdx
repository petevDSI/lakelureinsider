<QuickAnswer />

Rumbling Bald is the real draw: a free-access, 1,100-acre section of Chimney Rock State Park with an estimated 1,500 bouldering problems, plus traditional and sport routes. It's a separate thing from Rumbling Bald Resort down the road — same mountain, same name, different operator, worth knowing before you show up at the wrong gate. Two smaller areas round out the options, and two guide services are worth booking if you've never climbed outdoors before.

## About the Chimney Rock Closure

If you were hoping to climb the actual Chimney Rock formation inside the ticketed park, that program is on hold for now. The park's own site is direct about it and points climbers to Rumbling Bald instead — which, given Rumbling Bald's size and reputation, isn't much of a downgrade.
