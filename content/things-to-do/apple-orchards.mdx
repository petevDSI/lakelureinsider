Lake Lure and Chimney Rock don't have their own orchards, but you don't have to go far. Chimney Rock Road — US-64, the same road that runs through Chimney Rock Village and up through Bat Cave — leads straight into apple country around Hendersonville, about 18 miles and roughly half an hour away. Five orchards sit directly on that road, so a picking trip is really just an extension of the drive you'd already be on. Five more, a little further toward Hendersonville and Flat Rock, are popular enough to be worth the extra few minutes.

<OrchardDirectory />

## Planning an Apple Picking Trip

Apple season runs roughly September through October, but it shifts year to year with weather, and which varieties are ready changes week to week — an orchard that's picked clean of Honeycrisp in early September might have plenty of Pink Lady by October. Most of these farms post real-time picking updates on their own websites or social pages, so it's worth a quick check before you drive out, especially for the smaller, Sunday-only operations like Creasman Farms.
