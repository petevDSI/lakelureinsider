<QuickAnswer />

The Hickory Nut Gorge packs two distinct experiences into a short stretch of western North Carolina: the dramatic rock formations and waterfalls of Chimney Rock State Park, and the mountain lake recreation of Lake Lure. Most visitors pick one or the other; the ones who plan ahead do both.
