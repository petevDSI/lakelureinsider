Chimney Rock Village's Main Street is the real shopping stretch — nine shops packed into a few walkable blocks, from a gem mine and jewelry counter to an old-school general store with modern outfitter gear. Lake Lure itself has less traditional retail, but it makes up for it with Lake Lure Station: one complex housing a candy shop, an axe-throwing venue, and three escape rooms, all under the same family ownership.

<ShoppingDirectory />

## About Lake Lure Station

Candy Cabin, Bullseye Axe Throwing Co., and Lake Lure Escape Games aren't three separate stops — they're one venue at 2414 Memorial Hwy, started by Cory and Thomasina Coile after the family relocated to the area. Candy Cabin came first, in June 2024, followed by the escape rooms and then axe throwing. It's a natural pairing for a rainy afternoon or a break between hikes: grab candy, throw a few axes, and book an escape room in the same parking lot.
