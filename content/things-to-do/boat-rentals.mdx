## Start Here: Lake Lure Adventure Company

If you want your boat day to give a little back to the community, this is the place. Lake Lure Adventure Company is locally owned and family run, and the family took a hard hit from Hurricane Helene. They are back on the water with fuel included in the price and a free Yeti cooler with every pontoon or ski boat rental. Choose your own adventure: cruise the lake on a pontoon, then switch to a Mastercraft for tubing, skiing or wakeboarding with a certified crew. Their season runs from Memorial Day through Labor Day, and every reservation is made by phone, so call and tell them what kind of day you want. Visitors on Tripadvisor give them 4.8 out of 5 stars across 134 reviews. Their listing is pinned first below with hours, policies and the number to call.
