Four hours on a pontoon quotes between $440 and $460 across three operators — which looks like a wash until you look at whether fuel is included. Lake Lure Adventure Company and Lake Lure Boat Rental include fuel in their rate. Good Time includes both fuel and taxes. We have not confirmed Rumbling Bald's fuel policy. Fuel is a real cost on top of a rental rate, and comparing sticker prices without that column produces a misleading ranking.

## Featured Local Pick: Lake Lure Adventure Company

If you want to put your money into a small, family-owned Lake Lure business, start here. Lake Lure Adventure Company is locally owned and was hit hard by Hurricane Helene. Fuel is included in the price, and every pontoon or ski boat rental comes with a free Yeti cooler. Their listing is pinned first below with hours, policies and a phone number to call. Reviewers on Tripadvisor rate them 4.8 of 5 across 134 reviews.

The comparison table below shows the 4-hour rate and fuel status side by side. Sorting by price alone will mislead you; the fuel column is the point.

One operator — Lake Lure Boat Rental — has two conflicting rate schedules on their site (one shows 4 hours at $460, the other at $350). Both are marked unconfirmed. Call them directly before booking; do not assume either rate is current.
