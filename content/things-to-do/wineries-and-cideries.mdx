Like the apple orchards, none of these are inside Lake Lure or Chimney Rock itself — but several sit close enough, along the same Chimney Rock Road corridor toward Hendersonville and Edneyville, that they pair naturally with a day trip out that way. One well-known name is missing from the list: Burntshirt Vineyards' own tasting room on Main Street in Chimney Rock has been closed since Hurricane Helene, so we're pointing to their main Hendersonville location instead, which is open and where the wine is actually made.

## About the Chimney Rock Location

If you remember Burntshirt's tasting room and bistro at 438 Main Street in Chimney Rock, it hasn't reopened. Burntshirt's own site states it plainly: the location "will remain closed for the season" following Hurricane Helene's impact on the Lake Lure and Chimney Rock communities, with no reopening date given as of our last check. The winery itself is unaffected — it's still producing and pouring at its Hendersonville property, 2695 Sugarloaf Road, which is the tasting room we list above.
