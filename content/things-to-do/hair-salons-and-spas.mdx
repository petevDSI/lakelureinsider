Lake Lure has more personal-care options in town than you'd expect for its size — a full-service hair and nail salon plus a resort spa, both open to visitors, not just residents or club members. For anything more specialized, Rutherfordton (the same town that comes up on our [health & emergency services page](/trip-planning/health-and-emergency-services)) has the nearest dedicated nail salon and barbershop.

This is a neutral directory, not a ranking — Lake Lure Insider has no affiliation with, and takes no payment from, any business listed here.

<InsiderTip>
The historic Lake Lure Inn's own spa has conflicting information about whether it's currently open — its own site invites bookings, but the Inn's main site says it's temporarily closed. Call ahead before building a trip around it. The Rumbling Bald spa and Lake Lure Salon & Spa are the two safer bets if you want to book something without the uncertainty.

## Booking Ahead

Both the Rumbling Bald spa and Lake Lure Massage take appointments rather than walk-ins, and Lake Lure Salon & Spa is a small operation with limited weekly hours (closed Sunday and Monday) — for any of the three, especially during a busy weekend or fall foliage season, call or book a few days ahead rather than assuming you can walk in.

As of early September 2026, the Rumbling Bald spa announced that its massage license had been reapproved by the NC Board of Massage and Bodywork Therapy after a pause, with massage appointments resuming Saturday, September 5, 2026 — worth knowing if you tried to book a massage there recently and were turned away.
