## Where the record runs out

Some of what's in our reporting isn't a document at all — it's an account, and we've labeled it that way every time it appears: this reporter's own eyewitness notes from meetings attended in person; a transcript of Wednesday's meeting supplied directly to this outlet; private conversations Paul Brock has had directly with this reporter; and claims either side has made publicly that we haven't been able to independently verify against a document. We've tried to be explicit, article by article, about which is which — see each piece's own "Sourcing Note" section for the specifics. If you have a document that fills a gap here — the original Sept. 6, 2023 minutes as posted online, the Aug. 13 or Aug. 15 court filings, or anything else — we'd genuinely like to see it.
