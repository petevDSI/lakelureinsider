Chimney Rock Village has two new council members. With its single precinct reporting, unofficial results from the North Carolina State Board of Elections show Nicholas Sottile and W. Bennett Phillips winning the two open seats on the Village Council.

**Chimney Rock Village Council (vote for two)**

- Nicholas Sottile: 43 (39.81%) — *elected*
- W. Bennett Phillips: 36 (33.33%) — *elected*
- Richard Duffy: 26 (24.07%)
- Write-in votes: 3 (2.78%)

Phillips finished 10 votes ahead of Duffy for the second seat. More than half of Sottile's and Phillips' votes came on Election Day itself. The results are unofficial until Rutherford County's canvass at 11 a.m. Friday, Nov. 13.

## Two open seats

Neither seat had an incumbent on the ballot. Councilman Bruce Godzik could not run again because he no longer had an active residence in the village; his property was washed away in Hurricane Helene. Councilwoman April Schick did not file for re-election, according to [WCAB News](https://www.wcabnews.com/post/chimney-rock-village-council-tackles-recovery-budget-priorities-and-election-questions).

The new members will join Mayor Peter O'Leary, Mayor Pro Tem Buck Meliski and Councilman Patrick Warncke.

## Nick Sottile

Sottile owns three village businesses: Chimney Rock Smokehouse, the Broad River Inn and Chimney Rock Adventure Golf. He and his wife, Kristen, are the innkeepers of the historic Broad River Inn, which Helene left with a badly damaged foundation, according to a GoFundMe campaign friends started in October 2024. WLOS photographed the inn in February during its post-Helene renovations.

He already has a seat at the village table: Sottile serves on the Village Planning Board. This was his second run for council. In November 2024 he drew 25 votes and fell short of a seat, according to [The Digital Courier](https://www.thedigitalcourier.com/archives/oleary-meliski-keep-chimney-rock-village-council-seats/article_9b89b284-1a56-56c6-9227-f5da6bf53be4.html). Tonight he finished first.

## Bennett Phillips

Phillips kept a lower profile in the race and published no campaign website or platform. State voting records cited by WCAB News show he has voted in North Carolina elections going back to 1996.

## What comes next

Sottile and Phillips will be sworn in once the county certifies the results. Lake Lure Insider is asking both winners for comment and will add their answers when they arrive.

Chimney Rock is still in the middle of its rebuild, with sewer and plaza repairs targeted for Memorial Day 2027, as we [reported last month](/news/chimney-rock-council-sewer-contract-recovery-funding). The two new council members will help see that work through. Congratulations to both, and thank you to Richard Duffy for running.
