Ingles Markets, right on Highway 9, is the only full grocery store inside Lake Lure — and it covers more than groceries. Deli, bakery, floral, a gas station out front, and a full pharmacy inside. If you're renting a cabin for a week or staying at a lakefront lodge, this is almost certainly where you'll end up for basics.

## About the Pharmacy

Ingles' in-store pharmacy is the only pharmacy actually inside Lake Lure — but we cover it separately, on our [health & emergency services page](/trip-planning/health-and-emergency-services), alongside the nearest clinic, hospital, and dentist. Same building, different counter, different hours: the grocery side stays open later than the pharmacy counter does.
