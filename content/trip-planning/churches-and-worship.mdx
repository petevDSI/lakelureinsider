Lake Lure and Chimney Rock village are small, but there's more church variety packed in than you'd expect — several Baptist congregations, a non-denominational chapel, and an Episcopal parish are all within a few minutes of downtown. If you're looking for a denomination that isn't represented locally, Forest City and Rutherfordton (the same towns that come up on our [health & emergency services page](/trip-planning/health-and-emergency-services)) are the nearest options.

This list is a neutral directory, not a ranking — Lake Lure Insider has no affiliation with, and takes no payment from, any church listed here. Addresses and phone numbers are checked directly against each church's own website where one exists; a few smaller congregations only have a Town of Lake Lure or Chamber of Hickory Nut Gorge directory listing, which is flagged on that entry.

<InsiderTip>
Chimney Rock Baptist's outdoor Lakeside service (Easter through October) is worth knowing about even if you're not planning to attend — it's a good example of how much the schedule shifts seasonally around here. Call ahead before a first visit, especially around Easter and Christmas.

## Visiting As a Guest

Casual mountain dress is the norm at nearly all of these churches — nobody will blink at a visitor showing up straight from a hike or the lake. If you have young kids, Fairfield Mountains Chapel and the Episcopal Church of the Transfiguration both run a Sunday school program during the main service; call ahead if that matters for your visit.

For an extended stay, several of these churches also run a midweek gathering (Lake Lure Baptist's Wednesday evening service, Bat Cave Baptist's Wednesday evening service, and the Transfiguration's Thursday healing service) if a once-a-week visit doesn't fit your schedule.
