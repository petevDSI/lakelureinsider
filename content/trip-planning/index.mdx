  - q: "When is the best time to visit Lake Lure and Chimney Rock?"
    a: "Late September through early November for fall foliage — the gorge turns orange and gold and the views from the summit are exceptional. Spring (April–May) is quieter with wildflowers. Summer is busy but good for lake activities. January–early March has unpublished hours at Chimney Rock — call ahead."
  - q: "How many days do you need for Lake Lure and Chimney Rock?"
    a: "One full day is enough to do both well. Chimney Rock takes 2–4 hours; Lake Lure adds a half day. Two days lets you be more relaxed and explore some of the surrounding area."
  - q: "How do I get to Lake Lure from Asheville?"
    a: "Take I-26 E to US-74A W, or take US-64 E through Hendersonville. Both routes are about 35–40 miles and take around 45–55 minutes depending on traffic."
