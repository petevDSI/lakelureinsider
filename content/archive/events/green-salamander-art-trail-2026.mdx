**This event has ended.** The Green Salamander Art Trail's 2026 season ran May 1 through August 31 in Chimney Rock Village — 33 outdoor sculptures on public display throughout the village as part of the area's ongoing post-Helene push to bring visitors back downtown.

At the close of the season, the sculptures were auctioned off at an End-of-Season Celebration, with proceeds benefiting the Hickory Nut Chamber of Commerce.
