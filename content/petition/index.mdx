**On August 21, 2026, the Town of Lake Lure issued a notice terminating Second Mountain, LLC's lease at 2654 Memorial Highway — the home of Lured Market & Grill, Lake Lure's best-known business — and demanded the building be vacated by the next day.** Paul and Cara Brock have said they won't leave.

We think that's the wrong call, and we think it was made the wrong way.

## Who Paul and Cara are

Paul and Cara Brock opened the original Lured Market in June 2019, running a small local market — beer, wine, snacks, souvenirs — out of an old gas station in Lake Lure. In 2024 they bid on and won a Town lease across the street, hoping to grow into a coffee shop with lakefront access and boat tours. The lease was signed August 29, 2024. Twenty-nine days later, Hurricane Helene hit and washed away their planned October grand opening along with much of the rest of this community.

They rebuilt anyway. Today Lured Market & Grill employs 24 people and carries product from roughly 50 local vendors — by the numbers in the Town's own record, it is the best-known business in Lake Lure.

On August 14, 2026, Paul and Cara sat through a seven-hour mediation trying to work this out. The Town sent its manager and two attorneys. No mayor, no commissioner — nobody who answers to Lake Lure voters — showed up on the other side of the table.
