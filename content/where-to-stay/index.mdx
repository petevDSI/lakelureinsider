The Lake Lure and Chimney Rock area has a range of lodging that matches most travel styles: the historic 1927 inn on the lakeshore, vacation rentals on the water, and smaller motels and inns in Chimney Rock village. The right choice depends on whether the lake or the park is your primary reason for being here.

Right across from Lake Lure's beach &mdash; the only mountain beach in North Carolina &mdash; **The Perch** is a three-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhome that sleeps up to eight, with sweeping views of Chimney Rock from two private decks. Start the morning with coffee on the deck as the sun comes up over the lake, then wind down later in the private hot tub downstairs.

Inside, you'll find a fully stocked kitchen, two smart TVs, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a bunk room with an attached game room (classic Nintendo, Golden Tee, NBA Jam) that keeps kids entertained on rainy afternoons.
