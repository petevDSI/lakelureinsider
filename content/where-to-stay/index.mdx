The Lake Lure and Chimney Rock area has a range of lodging that matches most travel styles: the historic 1927 inn on the lakeshore, vacation rentals on the water, and smaller motels and inns in Chimney Rock village. The right choice depends on whether the lake or the park is your primary reason for being here.
