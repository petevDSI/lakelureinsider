Three sources that visitors commonly consult currently give conflicting information about this area's status. This page uses only primary sources — the park's own site, the Town of Lake Lure, and the organizations operating each attraction. Verified dates are shown for every item.

## Chimney Rock State Park

<StatusItem name="Chimney Rock State Park" status="open" detail="Open daily · Gate entry 8:30 AM–5:30 PM (main season) · Park closes 7:00 PM" sourceUrl="https://www.chimneyrockpark.com/" sourceLabel="chimneyrockpark.com" verified="2026-10-06" />

The park reopened late June 2025, nine months after Hurricane Helene. All four main trails inside the paid Chimney Rock attraction are open: Outcroppings, Exclamation Point, Skyline (1.1 miles past Exclamation Point, closes 5:00 PM), and Hickory Nut Falls (closes 6:00 PM).

Separately, NC State Parks' own site lists the free Eagle Rock Access as "closed until further notice," with the Weed Patch Mountain, Tunnel, and Riverwalk trails closed and all bike trails closed park-wide. These are a different part of the broader Chimney Rock State Park property, not the paid attraction above — but if you were planning to hike or bike Eagle Rock or Weed Patch Mountain specifically, confirm status at ncparks.gov first.

### Reservations: Two Official Sources, Different Answers

<StatusItem name="Reservations" status="conflict" detail="chimneyrockpark.com says 'recommended for busy weekends.' ncparks.gov says 'timed-entry reservations are required.' Both are official." sourceUrl="https://www.chimneyrockpark.com/" sourceLabel="chimneyrockpark.com + ncparks.gov" verified="2026-10-06" />

chimneyrockpark.com says reservations are "recommended for busy weekends and holidays." ncparks.gov says "timed-entry reservations are required." Both pages are official sources for the same park, and they say different things.

The practical answer: book ahead for Memorial Day weekend, July 4th, Labor Day weekend, all July weekends, and October foliage weekends. It costs nothing to arrive with a reservation you do not need, and you will not be turned away for missing your window — the park accepts walk-ups when the gate is open. Annual passholders never need reservations.

## Lake Lure

<StatusItem name="Lake Lure" status="open" detail="Officially reopened May 15, 2026, after a 20-month closure — swimming and boating restored" sourceUrl="https://www.townoflakelure.com/" sourceLabel="townoflakelure.com" verified="2026-10-06" />

### Washburn Marina

<StatusItem name="Washburn Marina" status="limited" detail="Reopened July 2026 — day slips, floating boardwalk, and fuel dock all now open. Fuel pump card reader not yet installed." sourceUrl="https://www.townoflakelure.com/" sourceLabel="townoflakelure.com" verified="2026-10-06" />

The Town-owned marina reopened July 2026, 22 months after Helene. Day slips are open. The Floating Boardwalk has reopened. Additional improvements are still underway.

**Fuel:** The fueling station opened September 2, 2026 — 90-octane ethanol-free gasoline, $6.50/gallon, daily 10:00 AM–5:30 PM, weather dependent. The pump's card reader isn't installed yet, so bring a credit or debit card and pay inside the Lake Lure Tours office at the marina.

**Dock access:** Hot docks on the left side of the marina (facing the lake), via the pathway to the Lake Lure Gazebo, offer temporary loading and unloading only. Docks on the right of that path are reserved for Lake Lure Tours. The playground is on the left.

**Swimming is NOT permitted** from any Town-owned property, including Washburn Marina Bay and Pool Creek Picnic Park.

<InsiderTip>
As of September 2026, Lake Lure Tours checks in passengers at Washburn Marina (2930 Memorial Hwy) — from a temporary trailer, while its permanent building is under construction — not at the beach. This reverses where tours checked in earlier in the year, so double-check before you drive to the wrong spot.
</InsiderTip>

### Lake Lure Beach

<StatusItem name="Lake Lure Beach" status="closed" detail="Closed for the 2026 season as of Labor Day, September 7 · Expected to reopen late spring 2027" sourceUrl="https://www.townoflakelure.com/" sourceLabel="townoflakelure.com" verified="2026-10-06" />

The beach ran daily through August 11, then weekends-only through Labor Day, and the 2026 season has now ended. The Town's September news summary confirms water-quality testing came back "within normal limits" through the close of the season. Check back here each spring for the next season's opening date and hours.

**Admission:** Adults <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.admission.adult" /> · Seniors <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.admission.senior" /> · Children ages 4–12 <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.admission.child" /> · Under 4 free with an adult.

**Aqua Park:** New inflatable adventure course — not included with beach admission. 50-minute sessions starting at 11:00 AM. Ages 7+ <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.aqua-park.age7plus" /> · Ages 4–6 <Fact id="lake-lure.beach.aqua-park.age4to6" />.

**Water quality:** NC DEQ publishes live swim-water test results for the Lake Lure beach via the [Western North Carolina Recreational Monitoring Program](https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/water-resources/water-quality/beach-water-quality-monitoring). After 20 months of storm debris in the watershed, current test results are the honest answer to whether the water is safe.

## Flowering Bridge

<StatusItem name="Flowering Bridge Gardens" status="open" detail="Open free, 24 hours a day, year-round — 3070 Memorial Hwy. Bridge structure demolished August 2025." sourceUrl="https://lakelurefloweringbridge.org/faqs/" sourceLabel="lakelurefloweringbridge.org" verified="2026-10-06" />

The gardens at 3070 Memorial Hwy are open — free, accessible at any hour, year-round. New features include the "All Twisted Up" garden near the west-end kiosk and a new Education Center. Both the east and west banks of the Rocky Broad River are accessible.

The bridge structure is gone. Hurricane Helene washed away roughly 150 feet of riverbed on the west end; the bridge was declared unsafe and demolition began August 18, 2025. Decorative elements were salvaged and reused in the new gardens.

**Replacement bridge:** A pedestrian connector is under discussion. A FEMA Section 406 grant went to the Town of Lake Lure for infrastructure repair. The Flowering Bridge organization says "options are being explored" for a pedestrian connection. The Town's July 2026 news summary does not mention a replacement bridge. This is not a committed project — it has no confirmed funding, schedule, or decision.

**Parking** is very limited: a few spaces near the Education Center on the west end, plus along Boys Camp Road. Note: Boys Camp Road Bridge is under construction from the week of July 27, 2026 — the temporary bridge remains in use throughout, so the road stays passable.

The gardens are run by an all-volunteer 501(c)(3). Dogs welcome on a leash. Portable restroom on site.

## Recovered Rainbow Bridge Collars

The Rainbow Bridge — where thousands of people had attached memorial collars for pets that had died — was washed away by Hurricane Helene in September 2024.

Some collars were recovered after the storm, cleaned, and stored by artist Amy Wald. The Lake Lure Flowering Bridge organization maintains a **[full list with photos of every recovered collar](https://lakelurefloweringbridge.org/rainbow-bridge/)** on their website. If you left a collar at the Rainbow Bridge, that is where to look.

A new Rainbow Bridge is planned. The organization's FAQ gives some timing — [check their page directly](https://lakelurefloweringbridge.org/faqs/) rather than any date in third-party coverage, as details are still being finalized. There is no temporary Rainbow Bridge. The organization is not affiliated with similar projects elsewhere.

## Chimney Rock Village

Roughly a dozen businesses are currently open, including RiverWatch Coffeehouse & Giftshop. A number of businesses destroyed by Helene have not reopened.

**Parking:** The main street fills quickly — ongoing road and building construction reduces availability. Beyond Main Street, there is a public lot **next to Chimney Rock Smokehouse**. Village contact: 828-625-4456.

**Green Salamander Art Trail** — ended August 31, 2026; the 33 sculptures were auctioned at the End-of-Season Celebration benefiting the Hickory Nut Chamber of Commerce. [Read the archived page](/archive/events/green-salamander-art-trail-2026).

## What's Easy to Get Wrong

**The Flowering Bridge is not closed.** The gardens at 3070 Memorial Hwy are open, free, and accessible 24 hours. The bridge structure is gone — not temporarily closed, demolished. The gardens have new features and are worth visiting. What you will not get is the bridge photo. Most coverage treats this as binary (open/closed) and it isn't.

**Lake Lure Tours now checks in at the marina, not the beach.** This flipped in September 2026 — older coverage (including earlier versions of this page) said the opposite. Check in at the temporary trailer at Washburn Marina, 2930 Memorial Hwy.

**The beach is closed for the season.** It ran daily through August 11, then weekends only through Labor Day (September 7), and is now closed until spring 2027.

**Marina fuel is now available.** The fueling station opened September 2, 2026. Bring a card — the pump's card reader isn't installed yet, so you pay inside the Lake Lure Tours office.

**Reservations at Chimney Rock.** Two official sources say different things. Book ahead for fall weekends and holidays regardless — it resolves the ambiguity cheaply.

One widely-read regional travel guide currently shows incorrect hours, days open, and reservation requirements for Chimney Rock State Park. We mention this because that content ranks well and visitors act on it. Verify details directly at [chimneyrockpark.com](https://www.chimneyrockpark.com/).
